By sending an order, you agree to these terms. If you are under 18, please make sure a parent or guardian is okay with the commission and payment.

  • Once I have started working, payments are not refundable.

  • If I need to cancel before I start, I will refund what you paid for that order.
  • Chargebacks or payment disputes can end the current order and stop future commissions.

4. Working on your avatar

  • My delivery estimates are estimates, not fixed deadlines.
  • Missing files, late replies, platform updates, or a complex request can make an order take longer.
  • I will send progress screenshots when I can.
  • Small changes are okay. Big changes or a full redo can cost extra.
  • I will send the finished files on Discord or another method we agree on.

5. Your part

  • Please send clear references, the correct files, and honest details about what you want.
  • You need to own or have permission to use every base, texture, asset, and reference you send me.
  • Please reply in a reasonable time if I need an answer from you.
  • Keep a backup of the files after I send them to you.

  • You still need to follow the rules and licenses for the original avatar base and any assets.

7. Communication and privacy

  • Harassment, threats, or repeated disrespect can end the commission.

8. After delivery

  • I cannot control VRChat updates, hardware problems, third-party asset issues, or account bans.

9. Questions

If anything here is unclear, ask me before you order. You can email me at mystic189123@gmail.com.
